Web Typography and Color Basics

Why Readability Matters

Good typography makes a website effortless to read. Choosing a clean sans-serif font, setting an appropriate font size, and adding comfortable line spacing prevent eye strain for visitors.

Consistent spacing between lines and sections helps readers scan content quickly and understand the hierarchy of information on the page.

Building a Color Scheme

Limiting a website to three or four primary colors keeps the layout cohesive and professional. High contrast between text and background colors ensures accessibility for all users.

  • Use a light, neutral background to keep content clear.
  • Reserve dark blue and dark green for headings and strong emphasis.
  • Style hyperlinks with a distinct accent color and a visible hover effect.
